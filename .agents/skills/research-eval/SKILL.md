# Research evaluation

Read current `AGENTS.md`, `SPEC.md`, `PLANS.md`, [contracts](../../../docs/contracts/CURRENT_CONTRACTS.md), [sole protocol](../../../docs/evaluation/CURRENT_EVAL_PROTOCOL.json), and the existing task record. Work within the applicable [G0–G7 workflow](../../../docs/engineering/WORKFLOW.md), reusing its approvals and record rather than restarting orchestration. The M1 supplement references the sole protocol, without a second funding or Go rule.

1. Establish authority for protocol/material preparation, offline validation, historical reading or a specific live run. Configuration approval covers only its stated changes and checks. Model, Tokenizer, Browser, environment/database, dependency, purchase and external-write actions need their own authority. This skill does not implement a new runner.
2. Independently author and review six exact prompts, clarification cards, equivalence dimensions, critical failures and scorer-only references. Keep revealed input and scorer-only content separate. Do not reveal future prompts in progress messages. Freeze material/protocol hashes before one recorded seed; register actual product/mode, manual engine list, familiarity, code/templates/tools/quotas/prices and qualifications before revealing a task. Public material is not a true blind test.
3. First display starts active time. All reading, prompts, searches, source checks, rewriting and audit-triggered repairs count. Agent side uses the product plus human manual work, without external-AI repairs or deliberate copying of Control output. Control includes registered ordinary AI and one independent Deep Research per task. Keep waiting, audit labour and knowledge transfer visible. Final human quality, raw technical results and security/authority failures remain separate.
4. Retain every formal slot, including failures, cancellation, timeout and missing results; no replacement, topic swap or changed denominator. Six attempts share frozen versions. Compute paired savings and median only after all six final human reports meet common quality and active times are complete/nonzero. The protocol owns limits and the sole value threshold; this skill adds no release threshold.
5. Verify all hard conditions and decisive claims against original official sources with subject/version/region/time, minimal excerpt, UTF-8 locator, capture and report hashes. Quote/domain matches do not prove semantic support. Distinguish evidence-supported no winner from insufficient investigation. Quotas, failures and unprocessed alternatives cannot become sufficient coverage. Audit feedback identifies defects and original-report locations, not new research silently supplied by the auditor.
6. Link input revision, plan/work, physical call, source/claim relations, context selection, exact safe business request, response/usage/cost and report. Canonical records survive UI event caps; missing usage/cost remains unknown. Offline reconstruction only reads stored artifacts with networking disabled, never replays model/API calls.
7. Optimization retains a real symptom and trace, a falsifiable predefined target, minimum code change, fixed-source offline regression, independent review and separately authorized same-condition real retest. Record source/model drift and confounding. Failed experiments do not satisfy correction of at least one real symptom; changed input is a different experiment.
8. Historical B/G/O/current-3/9-8-12 materials explain only their original scope. Read explicit Git commit/path/raw SHA and explicit newline transform where required; preserve original r12, source, fee and authorization records. Historical reading grants no new ledger extension, reservation or execution.

Return actual commands/results, artifact identities, review scope and unresolved qualifications to the sole task record. Protocol preparation, validator checks, independent review, human acceptance, mastery, value, Go and release are separate conclusions.
